About GigaDevice
GigaDevice Semiconductor Inc. (SSE Stock Code 603986), founded in April 2005, is a leading global fabless supplier dedicated to developing advanced memory technologies, microcontrollers (MCUs), sensor, analog products and solutions. Through operations and distributor networks across China, Singapore, the United States, South Korea, Japan, the United Kingdom, Germany, Turkey, Italy, and Mexico, GigaDevice can provide customers with prompt and reliable local support.
