About GigaDevice

GigaDevice Semiconductor Inc. (SSE Stock Code 603986), founded in April 2005, is a leading global fabless supplier dedicated to developing advanced memory technologies, microcontrollers (MCUs), sensor, analog products and solutions. Through operations and distributor networks across China, Singapore, the United States, South Korea, Japan, the United Kingdom, Germany, Turkey, Italy, and Mexico, GigaDevice can provide customers with prompt and reliable local support.

  • The world's #1
    fabless Flash memory provider

    GigaDevice is the #2 SPI NOR Flash® supplier worldwide, with the shipment of over 31 billion units since its inception.

  • 7 Ranked in Global
    32-bit General Purpose MCU

    GigaDevice offers over 700 part numbers from 64 series to choose from, with the shipment of more than 2.5 billion units to date.

  • Specializing in
    sensor technology

    Deeply cultivate sensors, signal chain, algorithms and solutions, and be a key contributor to the smart ecosystem

Creating a chip ecosystem fusing computing, storage, and sensing

Embracing the smart networked chip age

GigaDevice's core product lines are Flash memory, 32-bit general-purpose MCU, smart human-machine interaction sensors, analog products and solutions. GigaDevice's industry-proven products are renowned for high performance and low power consumption.

GigaDevice has received the ISO26262:2018 automotive functional safety ASIL D certification, IEC 61508 functional safety product certification, as well as ISO9001, ISO14001, ISO45001, and Duns certifications. In a constant quest to expand our technology offering to customers, GigaDevice has also formed strategic alliances with leading foundries, assembly, and test plants to streamline supply chain management.
